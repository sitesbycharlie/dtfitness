Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. DT Fitness is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018.

Data Controller

The data controller for this website is DT Fitness, Drumrooske Business Park, Donegal Town, Co. Donegal, Ireland. Phone: (074) 972 5403.

What Personal Data We Collect

This website itself does not collect personal data directly. If you contact us by phone or in person, we may retain your name and contact details for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. If you use our LegitFit portal to purchase a membership or book a class, LegitFit processes your data under their own privacy policy.

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about how visitors use this website, including pages visited, time spent on the site, and approximate geographic location at country or city level. This data does not identify you personally.

Legal Basis for Processing

Where we process personal data, we do so on the following legal bases under GDPR Article 6: (a) your consent, where given; (b) our legitimate interest in understanding how our website is used and improving our services; and (c) compliance with a legal obligation where applicable.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to distinguish visitors and analyse traffic. These cookies do not identify you personally. You can refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to use this site.

How Long We Keep Your Data

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default. Any personal data you provide when contacting us directly is kept only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and is then securely deleted.

Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. Anonymous analytics data is processed by Google LLC under Google's Data Processing Terms. Google may transfer this data outside the EU under Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. We have no other third-party data sharing arrangements.

Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data: the right to access the data we hold about you; the right to have inaccurate data corrected; the right to request erasure of your data; the right to restrict or object to processing; and the right to data portability where applicable. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at (074) 972 5403 or call into the gym in person.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe your data protection rights have been infringed, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) at dataprotection.ie, or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
